# Remote Work Policy

_Applies to all employees who have completed probation · Updated February 2026_

## Hybrid work

Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week. Agree your home days with your manager and record them in the team calendar. Tuesdays and Thursdays are office days for every team.

## Eligibility

Remote work is available once probation is complete. Roles that need to be on site, such as reception and facilities, are not eligible.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, headset and monitor for home use. Return all equipment when you leave the company, and report IT problems to the service desk.
