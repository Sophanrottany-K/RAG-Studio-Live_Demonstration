KOSIGN

# Products, Services & Technical Capabilities

Full product pages, local business, outsourcing, infrastructure and public R&D

Research date: 5 November 2026

Scope: This document consolidates the readable text from the KOSIGN homepage, Local Business, individual product pages, IT Outsourcing and Infrastructure & R&D pages. Product descriptions and capabilities are attributed to KOSIGN's website.

## 1. Local Business Positioning

KOSIGN describes its local business offering as a B2B enterprise ecosystem integrated with Cambodian financial infrastructure. Its stated aims include operational efficiency and support for tax and government regulatory requirements.

## 2. Local Business Solutions

- WeGroupWare (Smart Office Engine): Real-time attendance tracking and customized payroll calculation, described as automatically synchronized with Cambodian labor law and tax deductions.

- WeUMS365 (Unified Messaging Platform): An omnichannel messaging gateway unifying local telecom SMS, Telegram and KakaoTalk through a single API.

- WeBill365 (Collections Optimization): Automates corporate billing and settlement by connecting virtual-account issuance with instant KHQR collection.

- WeInvoice (GDT e-Invoice Integration): An e-tax invoice engine described as synchronized with GDT specifications.

- WeLoan (Digital Lending Core): Digitizes the lending lifecycle, from application intake and CBC risk lookup to review and repayment collection.

- WeFinFlow (Integration Middleware): A gateway for local insurance data synchronization, commercial transaction mapping and bank API processing.

## 3. AI-Driven Business Transformation

- Legacy data structuring: Transforms fragmented internal data sources into structured knowledge assets and AI-ready data pipelines.

- Custom AI-agent scenarios: Consulting to design AI virtual assistants for repetitive workflows and real-time analysis.

- Regulatory-risk and security governance: Implementation guidance for legal, financial and tax audit requirements and safer AI use.

## 4. Individual Product Pages

### 4.1 WeBill365 — Billing & Invoicing Solution

A billing and collection platform covering invoice issuance, payment, automatic collection, deposit confirmation and settlement. The website says it uses bank accounts and QR payments, with real-time automatic matching.

- Intended users: Financial services; condominium and strata operators; logistics and distribution; property and real estate; education institutions.

- Recurring billing: Subscription, periodic utility and scheduled rental charges.

- Bank auto-collection: Direct corporate bank-account connections for automated real-time matching.

- KHQR payment: Dynamic Cambodian-standard KHQR generated within invoices.

- Direct debit: Automated collection based on verified customer bank authorizations.

- Settlement and reconciliation: Daily balance sheets, fee reconciliations and custom accounting export formats.

- Live product link: https://webill365.com/

### 4.2 WeUMS365 — Messaging Service

An omnichannel messaging solution combining local telecom networks, global messaging and social media through a web console and API integration.

- Intended users: Enterprises, SaaS platforms and financial institutions.

- Local telecom channels: Smart, Cellcard, Metfone and other local operators.

- Social messaging: Telegram, WeChat, Zalo and KakaoTalk.

- Messaging capabilities: Bulk sending, scheduled sending, real-time alerts, delivery history and API integration.

- Live product link: https://weums365.com/

### 4.3 WeGroupWare — Groupware / Back Office

An all-in-one back-office platform combining HR, assets and administration, described as designed for mid-sized enterprises.

- Intended users: Enterprises, SMEs, corporations, government organizations and NGOs.

- HR management: Centralized employee records, workforce status, attendance, overtime, leave and organizational administration.

- Electronic approval: Document drafting, organization-based approval routing, delegation, request-time numbering, department-specific form lists and form administration.

- Employee self-service: Personal home page, leave balance and history, monthly attendance, quick request forms, profile-change requests and company directory.

- Business calendar: Personal, team and company calendars; participant scheduling; automatic leave and overtime updates; leave planning; Cambodian public holidays.

- Notice board: Company boards, threaded comments, attachments, board administrators, favorites and thread locking.

- Administration and security: Multi-company tenancy, company and department structure, role-based access and audit logging.

- Live product link: https://wegroupware.kosign.com.kh/

### 4.4 Attendance Management

A cloud attendance and leave-management system combining employee self-service with an HR administration console for requests, approvals and organization-wide visibility.

- Intended users: Employees, HR and admin teams, department managers and approvers, and executives.

- Employee management: Searchable and filterable employee list with department, team, level, hire date and status; Excel export and printing. The product page's demo text says “All 135 employees,” which appears to be sample/demo content rather than a confirmed KOSIGN headcount.

- Department status and organization chart: Manage departments, assign managers and view employees by department.

- Attendance management: Review late arrivals, early leave, unregistered attendance and annual-leave usage.

- Overtime management: Track overtime requests and manage approval decisions through an approval inbox.

- Leave management: Manage leave requests and annual-leave balances.

- Attendance operations: Manage public holidays and attendance settings; the page says work on public holidays is counted as overtime.

- Overtime operations: Configure overtime rates and monthly overtime limits by employee category.

- Leave operations: Configure annual-leave policies and automatically generate annual leave each year.

### 4.5 WeLoan — Loan Origination System (LOS)

An all-in-one digital lending platform for microfinance institutions, banks and credit cooperatives. WeLoan connects application, credit scoring, disbursement and automatic collection in one workflow from initial application through final repayment.

- Intended users: MFIs, banks and NBFIs, credit cooperatives, digital lenders and microfinance NGOs.

- Key features: Loan origination, credit scoring, disbursement, collections and CBC checks.

### 4.6 WeFinFlow — Banking Solution

A digital platform for financial institutions combining insurance and core-banking connectivity, credit and loan management, reporting and compliance, risk management and financial analytics.

- Intended users: Banks, insurance firms, MFIs, financial institutions and leasing companies.

- Key features: Insurance, core-banking integration, credit and loan reports, risk and compliance, and financial analytics.

- AI chatbot: Answers FAQs from an organization's knowledge base and includes routing, escalation and chat history.

- Mobile native widget: iOS and Android home-screen widgets for account balances and QR payments.

- Mini App Platform: Onboarding partners and building mini-apps inside a banking app.

- WeCRM365: Centralized CRM for banks to manage customer data and interactions.

- E-Learning: Role-based course, quiz, certificate and approval management.

### 4.7 Smart ERP Solution / WABOOKS

The product page is titled Smart ERP Solution and links to the WABOOKS website. It describes a digital financial solution focused on expense and receipt management through mobile access, bank integration and permission controls.

- Intended users: SMEs, accounting staff and executives.

- Key features: Mobile receipt management, expense management, bank integration and permission management.

- Contacts inquiry: Searchable directory of companies, vendors and customer contacts.

- Contact details: Company information, receivable and payable balances, and contact persons in one screen.

- Quotation: Create quotations for a contact and track them by status.

- Purchase order: Create purchase orders for a contact and track them by status.

- Live product link: https://www.wabooks.com/

## 5. IT Outsourcing — Webcash Group

KOSIGN describes itself as an offshore R&D and outsourcing center. It says its development team combines Korean-language communication with IT skills, working under Korean managers and applying AI automation to support ongoing upgrades.

- Webcash — 15 engineers: Maintains AI-Kyungrinara and leads development and operation of the Webcash official website.

- Coocon — 32 engineers: Builds a global fintech data-scraping engine, operates real-time scraping-center controls and supports simple-payment development.

- Bizplay — 19 engineers: Maintains the bizplay V4.0 platform and develops and operates a next-generation cloud-based framework.

- Bplay Pay — 5 engineers: Designs and develops blockchain-based Bplay Wallet (stablecoin) and maintains the Bplay Pay mobile meal-voucher app.

- Madras Check — 5 engineers: Develops groupware integrated with Flow and designs unified billing-system architecture.

- Webcash Global / Global TFT — 8 engineers: Develops and operates WeCMS global cash management and WeTAX tax filing, designs Morningmate back office and performs SaaS QA.

## 6. IT Outsourcing — Cambodian Financial Institutions

- Shinhan Bank (NBC): Integrated banking services including CSS ATM network, Bakong QR transfer, RFT real-time transfer and NCS interbank transfer. The homepage states deployment at two banks and USD 100,000 revenue in 2024.

- NongHyup Finance: Mobile and web loan-management system digitizing application, review, disbursement and final repayment.

- KB Prasac Bank: Integrated banking infrastructure including ATM and QR transfer networks.

- PPCBank: Strategic partnership and core-banking system modernization/digital-finance cooperation.

- BNKC MFIs: Customized mobile application development for affiliated MFIs.

## 7. Domestic Developer Dispatch Program

KOSIGN describes a program dispatching Korean-speaking Cambodian developers to Webcash Group teams in Korea. The homepage states that 14 Cambodian developers have been permanently dispatched.

- Request: Korean development team defines required headcount and criteria.

- Selection: Candidate review, interviews and internal approval.

- Visa issuance: Prepare documents and obtain visas.

- Placement: Arrange flights and housing, alien registration card and team placement.

The homepage gives approximate process durations: around two weeks for short-term dispatch and around eight weeks for long-term dispatch of six months or more. KOSIGN says the program supports close collaboration, technology transfer, unified workforce management and a cycle in which returning developers strengthen local delivery capability.

## 8. Resource Control and Verification System

- Unified dashboard: Combines headcount and deployment metrics in a real-time monitoring environment.

- Revenue and settlement management: Calculates revenue based on engineer man-month performance.

- Tiered talent management: Classifies development resources into five skill-based tiers.

- K-Specialist control: Tracks technical domain, seniority and monthly deployment cost.

- Data integrity: Provides one-click Excel/CSV export for local backup and history tracking.

## 9. Infrastructure & R&D

### 9.1 Enterprise Hybrid Cloud — Build & Operate

KOSIGN describes designing and operating hybrid multi-cloud environments for mission-critical systems intended to run 24/7/365.

- Zero-downtime availability: Real-time load balancing and Multi-AZ redundancy.

- 24/7/365 monitoring: Unified monitoring of system resources, hacking threats, anomalies and proactive recovery.

- Local security and regulatory compliance: Hybrid local-data-residency and cloud governance aligned with local financial-authority and data-protection standards.

The cloud architecture illustration labels the internet, web application firewall, public subnet, internet-facing web load balancer, web servers across two availability zones, internal WAS load balancer, private subnet, active and standby databases across two availability zones, high availability, remote employee VPN access and internal network.

Named cloud service components include cloud virtual servers, cloud databases, Cloud SQL, file storage, web application firewall, messaging, monitoring, deployment, load balancer, data factory, cloud streaming and cloud DNS. The page also shows sample monitoring metrics such as request, user and error counts, 99.95% uptime, 18ms latency and 100% DNS health; these appear as illustrative dashboard values, not independently verified service-level guarantees.

### 9.2 Unified Standard API Platform

The API platform is described as a high-performance gateway with modular backend services for fund collection, real-time virtual accounts and remote e-KYC.

- Webcash-standard API gateway: High-speed API processing intended to minimize data loss and latency during traffic surges.

- Plug-in module architecture: Reusable modules for KHQR virtual-account payment, GDT e-invoice issuance and credit-score data collection.

- Security: End-to-end encryption and API token management.

- Financial APIs: KHQR generation, payment verification, settlement and reconciliation, and QR-scan payment.

- Messaging APIs: Local SMS, Telegram, WeChat, KakaoTalk and Zalo.

- GDT APIs: E-invoice issuance and management, e-filing submission, and payroll-tax filing/reporting.

The infrastructure page names client organizations including BNKC Cambodia Finance, BNKC Laos Finance, CK Finance, NongHyup Finance, BAMC Finance, Akaba Finance, BBU University, Maxima Finance and Hi-Tech Company.

### 9.3 Global-Standard Turnkey Outsourcing R&D

KOSIGN says it takes responsibility for the full delivery process, from prototype UI/UX planning and microservices backend development to QA.

- Korean PM-led collaboration: Senior Korean project managers coordinate milestones and delivery quality.

- Technology architecture: Use of open-source libraries and flexible architecture guidelines for scale.

- QA and handover: Environment compatibility checks, load testing and API documentation handover.

- Examples shown: Webcash Groupware (KRW 50M; boards, e-approval and My-HR); Coocon Billing (KRW 50M; billing, receivables, contracts and customers); CRMS 2.0 (KRW 30M; expenses, AR/AP, projects and vendors); Bizplay Billing (KRW 30M, marked planned; contracts, customers, billing, sales and collections). These amounts and project labels are reproduced as displayed on the website.

### 9.4 Cambodia Public-Sector Digital Transformation R&D

KOSIGN describes collaboration with Cambodia's General Department of Taxation (GDT) on a next-generation national e-invoice exchange core.

- National e-invoicing core hub: Design and proof of concept for a national administrative backbone with real-time, tax-law-compliant validation.

- G2B/G2C administrative simplification: Digitizing paper-based tax and financial processes through web and mobile portals.

- Technology sovereignty and talent development: Knowledge sharing and technical training for local government development task forces.

The page shows a GDT digital transformation roadmap for 2021–2035: 2021–2023 policy/legal framework and system build-out; 2024–2025 e-invoice rollout and B2G/B2B pilots; 2026–2027 full B2G/B2B mandate and compliance monitoring; 2028–2035 nationwide expansion and ongoing improvement. It lists KOSIGN as an E-Invoice Service Provider.

The page also shows a Ministry of Public Works and Transport (MoPT) Intelligent Transport System roadmap for school-zone traffic infrastructure: 2022–2026 policy/legal groundwork; 2027–2030 automated speed-enforcement pilot; 2030–2035 nationwide expansion. KOSIGN lists growing e-notice issuance, expanding overseas arrears collection using WeBill365 KHQR, and supporting Cambodia's digital transformation.
