## 1. Strategic Partnerships and Alliances
KOSIGN presents its alliances as partnerships with government and financial-sector organizations, intended to support
national digital innovation and deployment of B2B financial solutions.
Government sector
- September 2014 — H.E. Hun Many visit: The website says UYFC President and Member of Parliament H.E. Hun Many
visited Webcash headquarters in Seoul and met Cambodian trainees.
- December 2015 — KOTRA CSR Award: Webcash received the CDC Secretary-General’s Award at KOTRA’s 2015 CSR
Award, recognizing support for the Korea Software HRD Center and Cambodian IT workforce development.
- September 2025 — GDT e-Invoice: KOSIGN says it joined the Cambodia E-Invoice System workshop and signed an
MOU with the General Department of Digital Economy under the Ministry of Economy and Finance as an official e-invoice
system provider.
Financial-sector and technology alliances
- PPCBank — December 2022: Strategic partnership MOU to jointly market WeBooks (formerly WABOOKS), an
accounting solution for corporate clients.
- Blue Technology — July 2016: MOU to make POS-machine payments easier and more flexible.
- TrueMoney — August 2016: Bill-payment service MOU; KOSIGN describes becoming a Super Biller able to resell
TrueMoney bill-payment services to other billers.
- CloudNET — August 2015: Cooperation MOU between KOSIGN, described as an e-Finance specialist, and CloudNET,
an ERP systems specialist, to cooperate on current and future services.
The page includes image captions for the Hun Many visit, Cambodian trainees, commemorative gift exchange with Webcash
leadership, KOTRA/CDC award, GDT e-invoice license, and MOUs with CloudNET, TrueMoney, Blue Technology and
PPCBank.
## 2. Corporate Social Responsibility
KOSIGN describes more than a decade of social contribution focused on Cambodian IT talent development and local
community sponsorship.
IT education and talent development
- 2026 MoEYS Minister Award: The website says KOSIGN was recognized for contributions to national IT workforce
development and modernization of university computing infrastructure.
- KOSIGN Global Tech-CSR Academy: Fully funded advanced web/mobile engineering training for top computer-science
students at leading universities, described as linked to internal hiring.
- Digital education support: Computer-equipment donations, free facility renovations and coding volunteering for rural
schools and underserved children.
Community sponsorship

- Cambodia Wrestling Federation — since 2014: Sponsorship of the national wrestling team, including support for
training and competition.
- Raonjena Computer Class — since 2023: Sponsorship providing PCs and hands-on learning environments for
underprivileged children.
- Multicultural Family Children’s Choir — since 2023: Ongoing sponsorship of a children’s choir and its activities.
The CSR gallery also labels activities including K-HRD admissions and opening ceremonies, global talent mindset training,
professional interpretation assessment, trainee graduation, annual K-HRD homecoming, a Cambodia MPTC minister visit
and remarks, a Busan K-HRD alumni gathering, an AGI technology seminar and staff/trainee sports activities.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What is KOSIGN?
A: KOSIGN is a Cambodia-based B2B technology and fintech company associated with Webcash Group. Its website
describes local B2B solutions, financial technology, IT outsourcing, infrastructure and R&D.
Q: When was KOSIGN established?
A: The company page states that KOSIGN was established in Cambodia in April 2013 by Webcash Group and five
affiliates.
Q: What is KOSIGN’s vision?
A: The website states “Cambodia No.1 B2B Business” and describes a focus on digital finance and public-sector innovation
in Cambodia.

Q: What are KOSIGN’s main business areas?
A: Local B2B solutions, IT outsourcing, infrastructure and R&D, and public-sector digital transformation.
Q: What is WeBill365?
A: A billing and collection platform for invoice issuance, recurring billing, KHQR payment, auto-collection, deposit
confirmation, settlement and reconciliation.
Q: What is WeUMS365?
A: An omnichannel messaging service connecting local telecom networks and messaging channels such as Telegram,
WeChat, Zalo and KakaoTalk through web and API tools.
Q: What is WeGroupWare?
A: An all-in-one back-office platform covering HR management, electronic approvals, employee self-service, calendars,
notice boards, administration and security.
Q: What is WeLoan?
A: A digital lending/loan origination platform connecting applications, credit scoring, disbursement, CBC checks and
collections.
Q: What is WeFinFlow?
A: A financial-institution platform integrating insurance, core banking, credit and loan management, reporting, compliance,
risk and analytics.
Q: What is WABOOKS or Smart ERP?
A: The product page describes a financial solution for expense and receipt management, mobile access, bank integration,
contacts, quotations and purchase orders. It links to wabooks.com.
Q: What does KOSIGN do for banks and MFIs?
A: Its website lists banking-network integration, ATM and QR transfer systems, loan-management systems, mobile
applications and digital-finance cooperation.
Q: Does KOSIGN provide IT outsourcing?
A: Yes. It describes offshore R&D and software development/maintenance for Webcash Group affiliates and system
integration for local financial institutions.
Q: What cloud and infrastructure services does KOSIGN describe?
A: Hybrid multi-cloud design and operations, high availability, monitoring, security governance, API platforms, messaging
APIs, financial APIs and GDT-related APIs.
Q: What public-sector work does KOSIGN describe?
A: The infrastructure page describes work with GDT on e-invoice exchange and digital tax administration, plus a roadmap
reference to intelligent transport systems.
Q: What community activities does KOSIGN support?
A: The CSR page lists IT education, computer donations, coding volunteering, Cambodia Wrestling Federation
sponsorship, Raonjena Computer Class and a multicultural family children’s choir.
Q: What employee benefits does KOSIGN list?
A: Flexible hours, insurance, employee loans, family-event support, sports clubs, Korean-language tuition, guest lecturers
and a developer Master program.
Q: How can someone contact KOSIGN?
A: The website lists +855 23 900 530 and business hours Monday to Friday, 08:00–17:00. Its email appears masked in the
extracted page text.
## 6. AI Chatbot Grounding and Answer Rules
- Answer using the website facts in these documents. Keep answers focused on the user's question.

- Do not invent product pricing, customer terms, service-level guarantees, exact office address, current job vacancies, or
missing email details.
- Treat company statements as claims published by KOSIGN, not as independently audited facts.
- Distinguish a product's stated features from demo text, illustrative dashboard metrics and sample employee counts.
- Where the website's numeric counters conflict or render as zero, state that the published page text is inconsistent and
avoid guessing the intended number.
- Use page-level source links in answers whenever possible.
- If a user asks about current availability, pricing, legal eligibility, recruitment or a scholarship intake, recommend direct
confirmation with KOSIGN.
## 7. Extraction Coverage and Limitations
This package captures the readable English-language text from the current indexed KOSIGN website pages located during
research: homepage, company, local business, IT outsourcing, infrastructure & R&D, partnership, CSR, welfare and seven
linked product pages. It also records visible contact-form fields, navigation labels and selected image captions.
Important: The user originally supplied https://www.kosign.com.kh/. That URL did not load during the earlier retrieval
attempt. Search indexing and page navigation surfaced the current KOSIGN-branded site at https://kosign.dev/, which is the
source used for this extraction. Therefore this is a broad extraction of the accessible current indexed site, not a guarantee that
every asset, hidden page, dynamically loaded item, or all Khmer/Korean translations have been captured.
Some pages contain images and gallery items whose full visual content cannot be converted into text; image captions are
included where readable. Some numeric counters are malformed in the extracted page text. These limitations are explicitly
noted rather than filled with assumptions.
