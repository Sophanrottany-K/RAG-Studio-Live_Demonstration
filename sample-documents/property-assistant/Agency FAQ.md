Agency FAQ

Riverlane Homes is a fictional agency, and these details are samples for the demo website.

Opening hours

The Riverlane Homes office is open Monday to Saturday, from 8:00 to 18:00. The office is closed on Sundays.

Booking a viewing

To book a viewing, use the Book a viewing form on the website or email hello@riverlane.example. Tell us which home you'd like to see and when suits you. Viewings are free.

Agents

Sreymom Chan is the agent for villas in Chroy Changvar and Mean Chey. Dara Keo is the agent for family houses in Toul Kork and Sen Sok. Vanna Sok is the agent for apartments in BKK1, Daun Penh and Tonle Bassac.

Rent

Riverlane Homes only sells homes. All current listings are for sale, and no homes are available to rent.

Agency fees for buyers

Buyers pay no agency fee to Riverlane Homes. The seller pays the agent's commission when the sale completes.

Payment

All listing prices are in US dollars. Your sale agreement states the payment dates and the amount of the deposit.
