Home buying guide

Riverlane Homes sample guide. It is general information, not legal or financial advice.

Step 1: Set your budget

Decide your budget before you start viewing homes. Include the home's price, legal fees, transfer costs and any repairs or furniture you plan to buy.

Step 2: Book a viewing

Choose the homes you like and book a viewing with a Riverlane agent. Viewings are free, and you can see several homes on the same day.

Step 3: Check the paperwork

Before you make an offer, your agent shares the home's title documents. We recommend asking an independent lawyer to review them.

Step 4: Make an offer and pay a deposit

Your agent takes your offer to the seller. When the seller accepts, both sides sign a sale agreement, and you pay the deposit stated in it.

Step 5: Complete the purchase and transfer the title

You pay the remaining balance on the agreed date, and the title is transferred. Your lawyer can explain the taxes and fees that apply to your purchase.

Foreign buyers

The rules for foreign buyers depend on the type of property. Ask an independent lawyer which properties you can buy before you make an offer.
